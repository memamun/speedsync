Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers regarding device permissions, privacy, and background operation.

Why does SpeedSync request Location permission?
Starting with Android 9 (Pie) and reinforced in Android 10–14, Google's Android operating system treats the connected Wi-Fi router name (SSID) as sensitive metadata that requires Location permission. SpeedSync accesses this permission strictly on-device so it can display your Wi-Fi name (e.g. Home wifi_5G) in the notification bar. Your physical GPS coordinates are never read, stored, tracked, or transmitted anywhere.
Does SpeedSync drain my phone's battery?
No. SpeedSync features intelligent Screen-Off Conservation. As soon as your device screen turns off, the app automatically throttles polling loops and pauses all icon/bitmap rendering. In 24-hour real-world tests, SpeedSync accounts for less than 1.2% of total battery consumption.

Can I run SpeedSync without rooting my phone?
Yes! SpeedSync requires zero root access. It utilizes standard Android operating system APIs (TrafficStats and Foreground Services) and runs on any standard Android phone running Android 7.0 or newer.
Is SpeedSync really free with no ads?
Yes, 100%. SpeedSync is an open-source project published under the Apache 2.0 license. There are no paid upgrades, no ads, no trackers, and no subscription paywalls.
